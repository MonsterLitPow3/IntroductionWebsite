Animation Video [The Plagiarism]

動畫影片 [借鋻還是抄襲？]

This video explores how crowdsourcing helps curb academic plagiarism and boosts modern learning and work efficiency. It outlines the definition and types of crowdsourcing platforms, reviews the evolution of plagiarism detection tools from Turnitin to advanced systems like HAMTA, and analyzes their strengths and limitations. The talk also puts forward practical suggestions for businesses and educational institutions to properly utilize crowdsourcing, so as to uphold academic integrity and drive innovation in the 21st century.

本片探討群眾外包如何有效遏止學術抄襲，並為現代學習與職場工作帶來正面提升。內容介紹群眾外包的定義與平台類型，梳理從 Turnitin 到 HAMTA 等抄襲偵測系統的發展歷程，剖析各工具的優勢與不足。同時針對企業與教育機構提出可行建議，引導正確運用群眾外包，維護學術誠信、促進二十一世紀的學習與創新發展。

Digipet: Control and Pet Mode

電子寵物：控制與寵物模式

Digipet is an interactive electronic pet project integrating STM32 and ESP32 control. It includes two modes: Pet Mode (emotional interaction, status feedback) and Control Mode (sensor control, remote operation). Combined with a web-based control panel, it realizes real-time communication, action response and intelligent interaction.

電子寵物 Digipet 是整合 STM32 與 ESP32 控制的互動式電子寵物專案。 具備寵物模式（情緒互動、狀態回饋）與控制模式（感測器操控、遠端操作）。 搭配網頁控制介面，實現即時通訊、動作反應與智慧互動功能。

All-IN-ONE Drone Project

科技研究與虛擬無人機專案

This project focuses on UAV structural design, aerodynamic simulation and 3D virtual drone modeling. Using Blender for full 3D asset creation, mechanical assembly simulation and scene environment building. The research also includes drone flight principle analysis, power system matching and future application prospects in delivery, inspection and aerial photography. By combining a possible approach of an ALL-IN-ONE Drone with Glide+Fly+Dive Functions

本專案聚焦無人機結構設計、空氣動力模擬與虛擬無人機 3D 建模。 透過 Blender 完成完整三維資產建模、機械結構組裝模擬與場景環境搭建。 同時研究無人機飛行原理、動力系統匹配，以及在物流配送、巡檢、空拍領域的未來應用發展。 融合並製作一個多合一方案，展現奔馳+潛移+飛翔三合一無人機功能。
